Metal removes the tail. On c7i the kernel path's p99 fell from 62 to 51.5 at an unchanged p50: the hypervisor's share was the p90 to p99 band.

The NIC generation matters more than metal. c7i's 37.5 Gb card is 9 µs slower at the floor than c6in's, so F-Stack loses to the kernel there.

Fast card plus metal plus 5 GHz gives a 17 µs floor and F-Stack's TCP cost halves to 6.3 µs; FIX lands at ≈ 13.8 one way, 27.6 round trip.

This table is the clearest statement of where the remaining microseconds live. Read the first row for the headline: one way, half the measured round trip. Then the floor rows: the raw network between two hosts is 17 to 32 microseconds per round trip depending on the card and whether a hypervisor is in the path. Everything the engine adds is 4 to 7.5 microseconds per round trip and tracks the clock speed. The F-Stack TCP stack sits between the two.
